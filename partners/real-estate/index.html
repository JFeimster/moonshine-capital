INVESTOR FAQS

COMMON TRANSACTION QUESTIONS

What paperwork is needed for transactional double closes?

We require the executed A-to-B contract, B-to-C contract, title company contact, and proof of end-buyer earnest money deposit.

Does the end buyer need pre-approval?

Yes, the C buyer must have verified liquid cash or an unconditional hard-money loan commitment on file with title.

Can real estate agents earn broker points?

Yes. Commercial and residential agents can receive referral compensation on non-mortgage business capital lines.
