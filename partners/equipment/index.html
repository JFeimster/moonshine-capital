EQUIPMENT FINANCING FAQS

Can I finance private party equipment sales?

Yes. Third-party independent inspections and clean title verifications are executed prior to direct wire release.

What age equipment qualifies?

Most facilities finance equipment up to 10–12 years old. Specialty heavy machinery can be older if hours and mechanical condition verify.

Can startups or new LLCs qualify?

Yes, startups with strong personal guarantor credit (680+) or existing down payments can qualify for asset-backed leases.
