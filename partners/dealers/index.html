Does this affect our floor plan or existing lender relationships?

No. Moonshine Capital operates as a secondary alternative capital desk. You run your primary bank programs first; when they decline or stall, you route the deal to us.

What are the borrower minimums?

Minimum $15,000/mo in gross business deposits, 6 months time in business, and an active business entity (LLC or Corporation). Soft credit pulls only.

How are dealer invoices paid?

Proceeds are wired directly to your dealership's designated corporate account upon digital execution of loan documents.
