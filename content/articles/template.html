The Death of the Local Bank

By Moonshine Capital • December 20, 2025

Twitter LinkedIn

The era of walking into a branch and shaking hands for a loan is over. The algorithms have taken over, and they aren't programmed to see your potential—they are programmed to mitigate risk for shareholders who don't know your name.

For most business owners, this is terrifying. For us, it is the greatest transfer of opportunity in financial history. When the "Big Banks" pull back, the private market surges.

Why The Void Exists

Regional banks are tightening credit boxes due to commercial real estate exposure and liquidity concerns. If your business doesn't fit a perfect 800-FICO, 5-year-history, zero-debt profile, you are effectively invisible to them.

How to Capitalize

To win in this market, you must change your positioning. You aren't a solicitor; you are a liquidity specialist.

  • Target Declines: Position yourself as the second opinion for every business the local bank says "No" to.
  • The Bridge Strategy: Position private lending as a "Bridge" to get to the next level, not a permanent crutch.

The capital is there. It’s just not where it used to be. Your job is to be the map.
