Briefing / FAQ

Clear answers. No corporate BS.

Do I need to pay for GoHighLevel separately?
If you want to use the CRM features in the Rainmaker tier, yes. You will need your own GHL account to import our Snapshot. We provide the architecture; you provide the software engine. If you don't have GHL, you can still use the Scout tier which runs on Notion.
Where do the monthly leads come from?
We buy bulk data from enterprise providers (Apollo, ZoomInfo, etc.) and filter them specifically for businesses doing over $250k/year in revenue across high-approval industries (Construction, Medical, Transport). We drop clean CSVs into the portal on the 1st of every month.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Moonshine OS is month-to-month. If you decide to leave, you keep the CRM snapshot and any leads you've already downloaded. You just lose access to the weekly coaching, new lead drops, and community.
I'm brand new. Is this too advanced for me?
No. The "Scout" tier is built specifically for day-one beginners. It gives you the foundational tools and the exact path to your first commission check without drowning you in complicated software.
